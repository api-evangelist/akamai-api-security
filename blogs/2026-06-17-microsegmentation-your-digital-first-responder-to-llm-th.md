Ofer Wolf argues that generative AI has compressed the time from vulnerability discovery to active exploitation to roughly 9 hours, making traditional patching insufficient. He positions microsegmentation as a dual-layer defense combining continuous application isolation with automated threat containment, and claims Akamai's AI-powered approach accelerates segmentation deployment tenfold while reducing operational friction.
